<h2>
    <b>A Brief About Me ...</b>
</h2>

My full name is **Ganesh Belgur Ramachandra**. My primary area of interest lies in rendering techniques from computer graphics. Game engine architecture, shader writing, simulation and graphics device drivers form my extended areas of interest. Currently, I work at <a href="https://www.amd.com/en.html" target="_blank">Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)</a> on developing and maintaining open-source, Linux graphics software drivers for upcoming and existing Radeon chips under the <a href="https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa" target="_blank">Mesa project</a> initiative. My strongest skillsets are in C/C++ and modern graphics programming.

In the past, I worked on Human-Machine Interface (HMI) software development for the luxury segment vehicles by General Motors under <a href="https://www.questglobal.com/industries/automotive" target="_blank">Ramp Group</a>, a subsidiary of People Tech Group (acquired by Quest Global). As part of the global computer graphics software team at <a href="https://www.mpcvfx.com/en/about/" target="_blank">MPC Film</a>, I developed and maintained the CG hair, fur and feather grooming tool called <a href="https://www.mpcvfx.com/en/site-search/?_keyword=Furtility" target="_blank">Furtility</a>, which earned me several feature film credits. Please feel free to head over to my <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm10166225/" target="_blank">IMDB page</a> to see my list of Hollywood feature film on-screen credits.

In my leisure time, I enjoy watching films, reading books and technical blogs, working on personal projects, and spending time outdoors. I love modern and classical western instrumental music. Please feel free to share your valuable feedback on my blog posts and connect with me on Twitter, Mastodon, Linkedin or even via email.
